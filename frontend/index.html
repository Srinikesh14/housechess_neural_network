ABOUT THE AI

Two NumPy neural networks

Both models evaluate positions from White's perspective and use the same one-ply move-selection method. Their input representation and training supervision differ.

V1 · ORIGINAL

773 → 256 → 64 → 1

Activations: ReLU → ReLU → tanh

Inputs: 12 piece planes, side to move, and castling rights.

Training: Original game-outcome supervision.

V2 · STOCKFISH

838 → 256 → 64 → 1

Activations: ReLU → ReLU → tanh

Inputs: V1 features plus en-passant target and halfmove clock.

Training: Stockfish evaluations of positions from a 400,000-game subset.

V2 has different weights and features; it is not guaranteed to play stronger. This app's move choice evaluates each legal move one ply ahead—it does not use minimax search.
